Where to Sit

Four Distinct Atmospheres

Each corner of SENJA is designed for a different moment of the day.

yard

Courtyard Garden

Open-air gravel terrace under ficus and monstera foliage. Gentle Bandung breeze, pet-friendly tables, and morning sunlight.

apartment

Limewash Conservatory

High arched glass openings with terracotta floor tiles. Plentiful discreet power outlets for quiet morning writing or reading.

local_fire_department

Hearth Counter

Low timber stools facing our wood-fired hearth oven. Warm aroma of 36-hour wild sourdough and slow morning roasts.

menu_book

Quiet Reading Nook

Deep low armchairs with linen cushions, dedicated reading lamps, and an open shelf of Indonesian art and architecture books.

Planning Your Time

Good to Know Before You Arrive

chair Walk-Ins Welcome Always

We do not take reservations or online table bookings. Our sanctuary is arranged to welcome walk-ins comfortably throughout the morning and evening hours.

pedal_bike Arrival & Bike Parking

Secure bicycle racks are positioned directly at our courtyard gate. Street car parking with local attendants is available along Jalan Riau.

spa Dietary Care & Mylks

House-pressed almond mylk, oat mylk, plant-based chia bowls, and gluten-sensitive hearth sourdough options are available on our daily menu.
