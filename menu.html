Dietary Information & Preparation

We believe in honest, transparent food. Our culinary markers indicate recipe composition:

Plant-Based

Prepared without meat, dairy, eggs, or honey. Coconut milk and botanical reductions used for rich textures.

Vegetarian

Meat-free dishes that may incorporate pasture eggs, cultured butter, French raclette, or sheep feta cheese.

Kitchen Sourcing Note

Our bakery tends active wheat sourdough daily. While we offer oat and almond mylks, our kitchen handles gluten, nuts, and dairy.
