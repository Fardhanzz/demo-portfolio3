Every week has its rhythm — from unhurried Sunday tables to quiet morning coffee cuppings.

How to Inquire & Join In

We believe neighborhood gatherings should be grounded in genuine human hospitality, not locked behind digital ticketing apps or speculative paywalls.

storefront

1. Walk-Ins Always Welcome

You do not need a reservation to visit SENJA. Our indoor tables, conservatory benches, and sunlit courtyard are open daily for coffee, brunch, and conversation.

chat

2. Inquire at the Brew Bar

Curious about upcoming cupping sessions or group tables? Speak directly with our baristas on Jalan Riau 84. They can share roast schedules and seat availability in person.

3. Creative Concept Notice

This What's On calendar is a fictional design portfolio piece. Dates, sample prices, and seasonal tasting menus are presented to showcase modern hospitality UI design.
