The SENJA Concept

A Sun-Drenched Gathering Place in Historic Bandung

SENJA is a warm, contemporary neighborhood café tucked behind Jalan Riau's old brickwork and leafy banana palms. Designed as an all-day sanctuary, we welcome morning coffee purists, midday remote workers, and friends gathering for golden-hour toasties.

Our kitchen celebrates West Java’s highland fertile soils—featuring single-origin beans roasted weekly in Bandung, 36-hour cold-fermented sourdough from our wood-fired hearth, and fresh seasonal harvests prepared daily.
